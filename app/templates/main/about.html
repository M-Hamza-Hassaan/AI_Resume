About airesume

airesume is an AI-powered resume screening and ranking system that uses advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP), dual-model matching (TF-IDF + Sentence-BERT), Explainable AI (XAI), and algorithmic fairness auditing to match job applicants with job descriptions transparently and equitably.

Our Mission

Our mission is to streamline the hiring process by automating the initial screening of resumes while maintaining transparency through explainable AI and ensuring fairness through bias mitigation. This allows recruiters to focus on the most qualified candidates and helps job seekers find positions that match their skills and experience.

How It Works

For Job Applicants

  1. Create an account and upload your resume (PDF or DOCX)
  2. Our system parses your resume using spaCy NER and extracts skills, education, and experience
  3. Your resume is vectorised (TF-IDF + SBERT embeddings) and stored for matching
  4. Recruiters can find your resume when it matches their job descriptions

For Recruiters

  1. Create an account and upload job descriptions
  2. Our system parses and vectorises the job requirements
  3. Resumes are ranked using dual TF-IDF + SBERT matching with section-weighted scoring
  4. View detailed XAI insights, skill gap analysis, and fairness audit for each candidate

Technology

airesume uses state-of-the-art NLP techniques including:

  • Resume Parsing: spaCy NER + regex for entity extraction (skills, education, experience, contact info)
  • Dual Matching Engine: TF-IDF + Cosine Similarity (baseline) and Sentence-BERT all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (semantic)
  • Section-Weighted Scoring: Skills (50%), Experience (30%), Education (20%)
  • Explainable AI (XAI): SHAP-style score decomposition, skill gap analysis with matched/missing badges
  • Fairness Audit: Text anonymisation (mask name, gender, location, dates, college) and counterfactual bias testing
  • Database: MongoDB for storing users, resumes, job descriptions, and vectors
